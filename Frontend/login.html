Eventopia Guide

Eventopia helps colleges manage campus events from one place. Register your college, import club and student data, and allow clubs to publish events that students can discover and join.

For a quick demo:

  • Choose a role from the role selector.
  • Use the demo credentials that are filled automatically.
  • Clubs can use a club email or club name. Students can use a registration number or student email.
  • Select a college if you are logging in as a club or student.
  • Click Login to continue.

On the first visit, the website may take 30-60 seconds to respond because it is hosted on Render Free Tier. Please wait and avoid refreshing the page while the server wakes up.
